Our Story

Built around what's nearby.

VERDE started in 2018 with a small farm in Karjat and a question: what if a restaurant cooked only with what was growing within a few hours of its kitchen?

Today we work with twelve farms across Maharashtra. Our menu is written every Monday based on what arrived over the weekend. It changes — always.

"We don't believe in signatures. We believe in seasons." — Anika Desai, Founder
The Chef

Chef Rohan Iyer.

After twelve years in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, Rohan returned to Mumbai with one goal — to cook Indian ingredients with the same seriousness European kitchens give to their own.

His philosophy: respect the ingredient, master the technique, never overcomplicate the plate.
